Environmental Equity Dashboard

House Our Neighbors

This dashboard explores how environmental burden, zoning, and housing equity overlap across Seattle. It highlights patterns in multifamily housing, proposed zoning changes, and environmental health indicators.

How to Use This Dashboard

Use the Dashboard Views dropdown to switch between Environmental Burden Analysis and Current vs Proposed Zoning. Use the layer list in the upper-right corner to turn layers on and off and compare environmental conditions, zoning patterns, health outcomes, and transit access across Seattle.

Project Findings

  • Environmental burden varies significantly across Seattle neighborhoods.
  • Current multifamily housing is concentrated in specific environmental contexts.
  • Proposed upzoning areas can be compared against environmental burden indicators.
  • Health outcome layers provide additional context for environmental equity analysis.

Data Sources

  • Seattle Zoning and Comprehensive Plan Data
  • Washington Environmental Health Disparities Data
  • American Community Survey (ACS)
  • Washington State Department of Health
  • Seattle Transit Accessibility Data

Environmental Burden Analysis

This map displays environmental burden indicators, zoning patterns, health outcomes, and transit accessibility across Seattle. Users can explore how environmental conditions overlap with current and proposed housing policies.

Key Questions

  • Are multifamily zones exposed to higher environmental burden?

  • How do health outcomes vary across different environmental contexts?
